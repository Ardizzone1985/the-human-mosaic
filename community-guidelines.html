Community Guidelines

Every memory deserves respect. These guidelines protect the people, cultures, stories, and creative expressions that form The Human Mosaic.

1. Respect Every Human Story

The Human Mosaic is built from real people, real memories, and real experiences from around the world.

Treat every participant, culture, identity, relationship, belief, tradition, and creative expression with dignity and respect.

2. Share Authentic and Lawful Content

Submit only images and memories that you own or are legally authorized to use.

Do not submit stolen, impersonated, misleading, fraudulent, or unlawfully obtained content.

You remain responsible for confirming that you hold all permissions required for the people and material included in your submission.

3. Protect Privacy and Consent

Respect the privacy, image rights, and personal boundaries of everyone shown in your content.

Obtain permission from identifiable individuals before submitting their image where required.

If a submission includes a minor, valid permission must be obtained from the minor’s parent or legal guardian.

4. Content That Is Not Allowed

The following content is not permitted:

  • Hate speech, discrimination, or dehumanizing content
  • Harassment, threats, bullying, or targeted abuse
  • Graphic violence, cruelty, or glorification of harm
  • Sexual exploitation or explicit pornographic content
  • Content involving abuse or exploitation of minors
  • Illegal activity or instructions encouraging serious harm
  • Content that infringes copyright or other third-party rights
  • Fraudulent, manipulated, or deliberately misleading submissions
  • Private personal information shared without authorization
  • Spam, advertising, or unrelated promotional material

5. Artistic Expression

The Human Mosaic welcomes photography, art, drawings, cultural expression, personal stories, and creative work.

Artistic nudity may be considered in the Creativity Room where it is lawful, non-exploitative, respectful, and consistent with the artistic purpose of the project.

Explicit sexual content, pornography, sexual exploitation, or content intended primarily for sexual stimulation is not allowed.

6. Comments and Social Interactions

Comments should contribute to a respectful and meaningful community.

Do not use comments to insult, intimidate, provoke, discriminate, threaten, or repeatedly target another participant.

Disagreement is allowed. Abuse is not.

Likes, comments, and other social features must not be manipulated through bots, fake accounts, coordinated abuse, or artificial engagement.

7. Room Relevance

Contributions should match the purpose of the selected room:

  • Identity — roots, culture, heritage, places, traditions, communities, and personal identity.
  • Love — family, friendship, relationships, animals, passions, care, and meaningful bonds.
  • Creativity — art, photography, illustration, design, performance, imagination, and original expression.

8. Moderation

Submissions and community activity may be reviewed through automated tools and human moderation.

The Human Mosaic may reject, hide, restrict, or remove content that violates these guidelines, applicable law, third-party rights, or the mission of the project.

In some cases, participants may be invited to submit a replacement image.

9. Reporting Concerns

If you believe that content or behavior violates these guidelines, infringes your rights, or creates a safety concern, contact:

info@thehumanmosaic.art

Include enough information for us to identify and review the relevant content.

10. The Spirit of the Museum

The Human Mosaic is not built around popularity, perfection, or competition.

It exists to preserve the diversity of human experience through millions of individual contributions.

Every face, memory, culture, relationship, and creative act becomes part of something larger.

This museum belongs to humanity. Help us protect it.

11. Changes to These Guidelines

These Community Guidelines may be updated as the project evolves, new features are introduced, or legal and safety requirements change.

The latest version published on the official website will apply.
