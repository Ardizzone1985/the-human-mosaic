Project Vision

One Humanity. Millions of Faces. One Mosaic.

The Human Mosaic is a global artwork built by real people, designed to evolve from a live digital gallery into an immersive app and ultimately into a permanent physical museum.

Founded by Giuseppe Ardizzone, the project was created to unite people through identity, love and creativity.

Why It Exists

A shared artwork for a divided world.

The Human Mosaic was born from a simple idea: to create a place where people from every culture, country and background can contribute a small piece of themselves to a shared global artwork. Every approved photo becomes part of a living project that will continue to grow over time.

The project is also committed to supporting humanitarian and environmental causes through public donation milestones as the community grows. It is not only about creating an artwork, but about building something meaningful, transparent and lasting.

The Three Rooms

Three themes. Three human dimensions.

Identity

Who we are and where we come from.

Selfies, places, traditions, cultural symbols, roots and personal identity.

Love

What we love defines us.

Family, partners, friends, pets, passions and meaningful moments.

Creativity

Human imagination without limits.

Art, photography, drawings, concepts and creative expression.

Final Vision

A permanent visual snapshot of humanity.

The final goal of The Human Mosaic is a permanent physical museum containing millions of photos from every corner of the world, organized into three themed rooms: Identity, Love and Creativity.

A place where future generations can walk through the faces, stories, cultures, emotions and creative expressions of people from around the planet.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can I upload a selfie?

Yes. Selfies are especially suitable for the Identity Room when they represent who you are or where you come from.

Can I upload a family, couple or pet photo?

Yes. These contributions are ideal for the Love Room, which is dedicated to emotional bonds and meaningful moments.

What happens if my photo is rejected?

If a submission does not fit the guidelines, it may be rejected and you will be contacted with further information.

Will my contribution remain visible?

The project is designed as a long-term artwork. Approved contributions are intended to remain part of The Human Mosaic as it grows.
